# Sample raw brief (free-text front door)

This is the kind of unstructured input the intake stage must accept. It can be pasted
prose like this, an email, or uploaded documents (RFP, basis-of-design, MEP basis). The
extraction stage reads it into the structured brief (sample_brief.json), citing where
each field came from, and asks at the question gate for anything it cannot extract
confidently.

---

We're bidding a hyperscale data centre for a client in Navi Mumbai, greenfield plot
inside the notified IT park. Target IT load is 20 MW, Uptime Tier III, N+1 on the
electrical and cooling. Scope is turnkey: design, build, MEP and commissioning through
to Uptime constructed-facility certification.

Delivery: we self-perform civil and structure. Electrical and mechanical power train go
to turnkey specialist packages. Gensets are owner-furnished under the client's existing
Cummins framework, so we only install and integrate them. Fire suppression and BMS are
subcontracted.

Power position: there is an existing 33 kV feeder on the east boundary but the HT
substation is on our scope to build. Grid energisation is the client's critical concern.

Site notes: the approach road floods in the monsoon, so heavy deliveries and the genset
lifts need to land before June. Client wants phased handover, hall by hall, first hall
live earliest. Target ready-for-service is Q1 2027.
